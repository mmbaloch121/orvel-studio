Orvel Studio

Make and publish educational videos from your own computer

Orvel Studio is a desktop video studio. It runs on your Mac or Windows PC and helps you script, render and publish explainer videos, starting with the Urdu channel Dairy Pakistan.

What the app does

  • Turns a researched script into narrated, captioned videos and YouTube Shorts.
  • Renders everything on your computer; your projects stay in a folder on your own disk.
  • Uploads finished videos to your YouTube channel, as private or scheduled, only after you approve them.
  • Reads your channel's view and retention numbers so you can see which videos held viewers.

How it uses your Google account

When you click "Connect YouTube" the app asks Google for permission to upload videos, manage captions, thumbnails and comment replies, and read your channel's analytics. You can untick the optional permissions. The app talks to Google directly from your computer; no Orvel server sits in between. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Contact
