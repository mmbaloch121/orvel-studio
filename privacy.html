Orvel Studio

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Orvel Studio ("the app") is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what the app does with your data, including data from your Google account and YouTube channel.

Who we are

Orvel Studio is made by an independent developer in Pakistan. Contact details are at the end of this page.

Data the app accesses from Google

Only after you click "Connect YouTube" and agree on Google's screen, the app may use these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload the videos you approve to your channel.
  • youtube.readonly: read your channel name and ID, your video list and their status, and comments on your videos.
  • youtube.force-ssl (optional): upload captions, set thumbnails, edit titles and descriptions of your own videos, and post comment replies that you have approved.
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly (optional): read views, watch time, retention and estimated revenue for your own channel.

How the data is used

  • Your data is used only to provide the features you see in the app: publishing, captions, comment replies you approve, and performance reports for your channel.
  • Everything is stored on your computer, in the app's data folder. The login token is kept in your operating system's secure store (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows).
  • The app sends your Google data to no one except Google itself. There is no Orvel server, and we never see, sell, rent or share your data.
  • Your data is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.
  • If you choose to use an AI writing or voice service inside the app, text you send to that service (such as a script) goes directly from your computer to that provider under your own account. YouTube data is not sent to them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Orvel Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Disconnect YouTube in the app's Settings to delete the stored login token from your computer.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page.
  • Delete the app's data folder to remove all projects, analytics and comments it saved. Cached YouTube data is refreshed or removed within 30 days, as the YouTube API terms require.

Children

The app is meant for adult creators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
